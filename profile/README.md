# StealthCRX

We're building AI systems that operate in the physical world. Unstructured environments, real sites, hardware that has to work when conditions don't cooperate. We're a small, experienced team and we're moving fast. Stealth for now.

---

## Open Roles

**Embodied AI / Physical AI Engineers**
We're looking for engineers who have built and shipped vision-language models, action heads, or sim-to-real transfer systems. Not researchers who've read about it — people who've put models into hardware and watched them work (or fail) in the field.

**DevOps Engineers (Hardware-Fluent)**
We run edge compute on job sites. If you've worked with embedded systems, real-time data pipelines from physical sensors, or firmware in environments where uptime actually matters, that's the background we're looking for.

**Forward-Deployed Robotics Engineers**
Half your time is in a terminal, half is on a job site. You're comfortable debugging hardware in the field, collecting data, and feeding it back into the training loop. You don't need someone to tell you when something's broken.

**Field Operations (Hardware + Logistics)**
Managing sensor networks and robot deployments across multiple sites. Client relationships, logistics, and the operational backbone of what we're building. This role owns the physical side of the business.
